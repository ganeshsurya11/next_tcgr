About us

TCG Real Estate, a leading player in the real estate industry since its inception in 1996, has established a strong presence across multiple cities in India. Under the aegis of global conglomerate The Chatterjee Group (TCG), we have consistently delivered world-class commercial, residential and managed living projects that epitomise India’s urban growth story.


TCGRE designs, builds, and stands behind residential and commercial developments across the city. We keep design, engineering, and construction under one roof, which means fewer handoffs, fewer surprises, and a single team accountable from the first drawing to the final walkthrough. Read more...
